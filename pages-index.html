Receive in your browser

Plug in an RTL-SDR dongle and run capture and decoding right here. Watch the filtered spectrum, triggered waveforms, and live measurements.

Requires a WebUSB-capable browser, an RTL-SDR dongle, and your sensor serial number.

02 / Remote receiverIQ stream

Connect to a receiver

Already running Sparslog on another machine? Connect to its IQ listener to explore the signal and follow decoded measurements.

Requires a running Sparslog receiver with IQ streaming enabled and a browser-reachable secure connection.

Open remote viewer↗
